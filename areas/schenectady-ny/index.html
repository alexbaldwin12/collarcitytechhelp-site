Businesses we hear about most in Schenectady

  • Downtown restaurants and bars. Around Jay Street and State Street, theater nights at Proctors fill nearby restaurants. Evenings bring crowds, so online reservations and card payments matter.
  • Stockade neighborhood shops and rentals. The historic Stockade has small inns, rentals, home businesses and boutique services that depend on a good Google listing and a reliable booking page.
  • Little Italy and neighborhood food businesses. Family-run bakeries, delis and grocers have served their blocks for many years, and many are now adding online ordering and social pages.
  • Offices near the courthouse and downtown. Law offices, insurance agents and accountants work near the county buildings and keep client records in email and cloud folders.
  • Union College area and Upper Union Street. Service businesses near the campus, such as salons, cafes and tutors, see traffic shift with the academic calendar.
  • Trades and home services. Schenectady has a lot of older housing, so plumbers, electricians and contractors stay busy. Most run from a phone, text threads and a shared estimate email.

Common tech and security problems for Schenectady businesses

Show-night rushes

On big event nights, staff handle a wave of orders and payments in a short window. Shared tablets and unlocked screens are easy to overlook. Simple rules for who uses what can help.

Old accounts from earlier eras

Some Schenectady businesses have operated for decades and picked up old email addresses, old websites and old logins along the way. We help you list what still exists and what can be closed.

Out-of-date Google listings

Hours, menus and phone numbers drift. If someone searches before a show and finds the wrong hours, you lose the sale. Fresh monthly posts keep your listing active.

Family business, shared passwords

In family-run shops, everyone often uses the same login to the same email. A password manager and two-step sign-in solve this cheaply.
