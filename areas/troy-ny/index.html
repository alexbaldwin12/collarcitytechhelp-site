Common tech and security problems for Troy businesses

Busy Saturdays, thin staffing

Downtown Troy gets crowds on market days and event weekends. When owners are slammed, they reuse one password for the register app, email and social accounts. That shortcut is easy to fix with a password manager.

Shared logins on social and Google

Many Troy businesses let a friend, a cousin or a former employee post for them. Nobody remembers who still has access. A short clean-up list takes care of it.

Lookalike emails to small offices

Offices that handle invoices and client documents get fake payment requests and fake file-share links. Knowing the usual signs helps staff pause before clicking.

Student and seasonal turnover

With colleges nearby, many Troy employers hire students who come and go. Each new hire needs accounts, and each departure needs them closed. Few small shops have a simple routine for it.
