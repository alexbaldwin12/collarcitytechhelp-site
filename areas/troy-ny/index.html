Businesses we hear about most in Troy

  • Restaurants, cafes and bars. Downtown Troy around River Street and Monument Square is packed with places to eat and drink. Many run online ordering, reservation tools and a shared social media login that several staff know.
  • Shops and studios. Independent retail, vintage, art and gift stores line the downtown blocks. Plenty take card payments and run a small online store on the side.
  • Professional offices. Law, accounting, insurance and real estate offices cluster in and around downtown and near the courthouse area. They handle client names, emails and documents every day.
  • Health and wellness practices. Small dental, chiropractic and therapy offices near the hospital and college campuses keep appointment and contact details that need careful handling.
  • Lansingburgh storefronts. North Troy's Lansingburgh neighborhood has its own mix of barbershops, delis, salons and corner stores that depend on neighbors finding them online.
  • Contractors and trades. Builders, restorers and trades working on Troy's many historic brick buildings often run the business from a phone, email and a Google listing.

Common tech and security problems for Troy businesses

Busy Saturdays, thin staffing

Downtown Troy gets crowds on market days and event weekends. When owners are slammed, they reuse one password for the register app, email and social accounts. A password manager fixes that shortcut easily.

Shared logins on social and Google

Many Troy businesses let a friend, a cousin or a former employee post for them. Nobody remembers who still has access. A short clean-up list fixes it.

Lookalike emails to small offices

Offices that handle invoices and client documents get fake payment requests and fake file-share links. Knowing the usual signs helps staff pause before clicking.

Seasonal turnover

With colleges nearby, many Troy employers hire seasonal staff who come and go. Each new hire needs accounts, and each departure needs them closed. Few small shops have a simple routine for it.
