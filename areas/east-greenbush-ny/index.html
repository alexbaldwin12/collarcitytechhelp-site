Common tech and security problems for East Greenbush businesses

Review pages that nobody watches

A one-star review on Google can sit unanswered for weeks when the owner is busy on a job. A calm, polite reply shows other customers that someone is paying attention. We write reply drafts you can paste in.

Business and personal email mixed together

Many owners use a personal webmail address for quotes and invoices. If that account is breached, both work and family are exposed. A separate business address with two-step sign-in is a clean fix.

No backup for the phone that runs everything

For a trade or a takeout shop, the phone holds contacts, photos, and schedules. If it breaks or goes missing, that information is gone. Turning on automatic backup takes a few minutes.

Fake vendor and supplier messages

Auto shops and contractors regularly order parts and materials, which makes fake invoices and changed payment details believable. A habit of verifying by phone before paying stops most of them.
