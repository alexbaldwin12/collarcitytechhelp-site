Businesses we hear about most in East Greenbush

  • Auto repair and highway-corridor services. The Columbia Turnpike has long had garages, gas stations and quick stops. They depend on reviews and an accurate map listing for drivers searching on the road.
  • Pizzerias, diners, and takeout. Neighborhood food spots take phone, online and delivery-app orders. A hijacked social page or a fake order email hits them directly.
  • Contractors and home trades. Builders, roofers, electricians and landscapers working across Rensselaer County keep quotes, photos and customer details on phones and personal email.
  • Dental, chiropractic, and small medical offices. Local practices keep patient reminders and contact lists. We give general security guidance only and do not provide HIPAA advice.
  • Child care, tutoring, and family services. East Greenbush is a family-oriented town, and providers talk with parents daily by email, text and social media. Those accounts need decent protection.

Common tech and security problems for East Greenbush businesses

Review pages that nobody watches

A one-star review on Google can sit unanswered for weeks when the owner is busy on a job. A calm, polite reply shows other customers that someone is paying attention. We write reply drafts you can paste in.

Business and personal email mixed together

Many owners use a personal webmail address for quotes and invoices. If it is breached, work and family are both exposed. A separate business address with two-step sign-in fixes that.

No backup for the phone that runs everything

For a trade or a takeout shop, the phone holds contacts, photos and schedules. If it breaks or goes missing, that information is gone. Turning on automatic backup takes a few minutes.

Fake vendor and supplier messages

Auto shops and contractors order parts and materials all the time, so fake invoices and changed payment details look believable. Calling to verify before you pay stops most of them.
