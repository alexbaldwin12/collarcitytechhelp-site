Businesses we hear about most in Waterford

  • Waterfront cafes and restaurants. Places near the canal and river see boaters, cyclists and day visitors in the warmer months. Online ordering, reservation emails and a correct Google listing all need to hold up when the crowds show up.
  • Canal and tourism-related shops. Small gift, outfitter and seasonal businesses near the locks live on a few busy months. A hacked email or a wrong set of hours then costs more than it would in February.
  • Home-service trades. Electricians, plumbers, landscapers and builders working across the village and the surrounding Saratoga County towns often run everything from one phone and one email account.
  • Home-based and one-person offices. Bookkeepers, designers, consultants and insurance agents who work from a spare room still hold client files and invoices that deserve a proper lock.
  • Salons, barbers, and personal care. Booking apps, a shared tablet at the front desk and a business Facebook page are common here. Each one has a login worth protecting.

Common tech and security problems for Waterford businesses

One email account runs the whole business

In a small village shop, the owner's email often handles orders, invoices and password resets for everything else. If someone breaks into it, they can reach almost every other account. Two-step sign-in on that one inbox is the most valuable fix most owners can make.

Seasonal hours and listings go stale

Waterford businesses change hours when the canal season opens and closes. Customers who see old hours on Google or Facebook drive over to a locked door and leave a bad review. A short monthly check keeps those listings honest.

Shared logins on the front-desk device

When a tablet or laptop is shared between family members or seasonal helpers, passwords get written on notes or reused. A password manager and separate logins fix this without costing much.

Fake invoice and payment emails

Small businesses of every size get emails that look like a supplier asking for a changed bank detail or an urgent payment. Knowing the usual signs and calling a known number first stops most of them.
