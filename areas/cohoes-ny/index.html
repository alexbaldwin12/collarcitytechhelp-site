Common tech and security problems for Cohoes businesses

One person does everything

In a small Cohoes shop, the owner is also the IT department. Updates get skipped when the lunch rush hits. A short monthly checklist keeps the basics from slipping.

Personal and business mixed together

Home-based businesses often use the same email and phone for family and customers. If that account is compromised, both sides are affected. Separate accounts and two-step sign-in are a low-cost fix.

Seasonal swings

Warm-weather visitors and local events create busy stretches followed by quiet ones. Listings go stale in the quiet stretch, right when owners need them for the next push.

Shared Wi-Fi in old buildings

Mill-style buildings often share one network among several tenants. Changing default router settings and separating guest Wi-Fi is an easy improvement.
