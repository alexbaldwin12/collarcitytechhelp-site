Common tech and security problems for Latham businesses

A shared office network nobody owns

In a multi-tenant office park, the wifi and printer setup may have been done once and never reviewed. Guest access, old passwords, and former employees can all linger. A simple checklist of who has access to what is a good first step.

Email spoofing that targets clients

Offices that send invoices and documents to clients are prime targets for fake emails that appear to come from their own domain. Three public email settings called SPF, DKIM, and DMARC help stop that, and we check them for you.

Client files in too many places

Files end up in email attachments, personal cloud accounts, and USB drives. When a team member leaves or a laptop is lost, nobody is sure what went with it. Pick one home for client files and back it up.

Staff who have never had a quick briefing

A new hire may get a laptop and a login on day one with no talk about phishing or password habits. A one-page staff guide takes ten minutes to read and covers the common traps.
