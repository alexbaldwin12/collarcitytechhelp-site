Common tech and security problems for Clifton Park businesses

The owner is also the IT department

A contractor or practice owner often sets up email, the website and payments after hours with no one to double check. Small gaps like reused passwords can go unnoticed for years. A written list makes them visible.

Competing with big retail for local search

Customers near the Exit 9 area search on their phones and pick whoever looks active. A listing with old photos, no recent posts and unanswered reviews loses to a busier one. A simple monthly routine closes the gap.

Wire and invoice scams in home deals

Real estate and renovation work involve big payments and tight timelines. Scammers copy a real email thread and change the bank details. The defense is a habit: confirm any payment change by calling a number you already trust.

Customer data on personal devices

Many Clifton Park owners work from phones, home laptops and tablets the family also uses. A lost phone or an unlocked laptop can expose customer details. Screen locks and backups are cheap fixes.
