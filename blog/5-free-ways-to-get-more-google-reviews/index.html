5 Free Ways to Get More Google Reviews for Your Small Business

By Alex Baldwin, Collar City Tech Help. AI-assisted, reviewed by a UAlbany cybersecurity student. Published October 8, 2026.

When someone in Troy or the Capital Region searches for a local business, Google reviews are often what they read before they call. The good news is that getting more of them costs nothing. Here are five simple ways to do it, all within Google's rules.

Google changes its policies from time to time, so check the current rules in your Google Business Profile help pages. Never write fake reviews, and never pay for them.

1. Get your direct review link

In your Google Business Profile, open the option to get more reviews and copy the short link. Anyone who opens it lands on a box where they can write a review. Save this link in your phone notes so it is always ready to send.

2. Ask at the right moment

The best time to ask is right after someone says thank you, or right after you finish a job. A simple text works well:

"Hi [name], thanks again for choosing us. If you have a minute, an honest Google review really helps a small local business like ours. Here's the link: [your link]. Thank you!"

Notice it asks for an honest review, not a five-star one. Do not offer discounts or gifts in exchange for a review, and do not only ask people you think will be happy. Google does not allow either.

3. Make it easy with a QR code

Turn your review link into a QR code using a free generator, then print it on a receipt, counter card, or invoice. Customers scan it with their phone camera and land on the review box. Test the code yourself before you print a hundred copies.

4. Reply to every review

Thank people who say nice things, and answer critical reviews calmly and briefly. Do not argue or share private details. Replies show future customers that a real person is paying attention, and many owners find that it encourages more people to write.

5. Ask a few people every week, not everyone once

A steady trickle of fresh reviews looks more natural and more useful than a burst of twenty in one day. Pick a small habit, such as asking two or three happy customers each week, and keep going.

A quick safety note

Review requests are a favorite cover for scammers. Be careful with emails or texts that claim your Google Business Profile will be suspended unless you click a link, or that offer to sell you reviews. Go to your profile directly instead of clicking.
