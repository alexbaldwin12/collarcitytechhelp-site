Refund Policy

Last updated 2026-10-09. Collar City Tech Help, Mail: PO Box 182, Waterford, NY 12188.

Plain-English summary, not legal advice. Have a qualified professional review before relying on it.

Our promise

If you are not satisfied, you can get a full refund within 7 days. This covers all three of our paid offers: the Security Scorecard ($79), the Online Presence plan ($99/month), and the Starter Kit ($24). You do not need to explain in detail. Payment is by card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay through our secure Stripe checkout.

When the 7 days start

  • Security Scorecard and Starter Kit: 7 days from the date of purchase.
  • Online Presence plan: 7 days from your first charge.
  • Later monthly charges are not refunded just because a month has passed. You can cancel any time to stop future charges.

How to ask for a refund

Email collarcitytechhelp@gmail.com from the email address you used to order. Put 'Refund' in the subject and include the product name. You can also write to Collar City Tech Help, PO Box 182, Waterford, NY 12188, though email is faster.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. After we issue a refund, it usually shows up in 5 to 10 days, depending on your bank or card company. Apple Pay and Google Pay refunds go to the card behind them. You will get an email when we issue it.

Canceling the monthly plan

Cancelling is separate from a refund. You can cancel the Online Presence plan any time by emailing collarcitytechhelp@gmail.com from your order email or replying "cancel" to any email from us. If you cancel, you will not be charged again, and the plan stays active until the end of the month you paid for. A refund request inside the 7-day window also ends the plan.

If you are unsure whether to cancel or ask for a refund, tell us and we will explain the options.

If something went wrong

If a report or pack arrives late, contains a mistake, or misses what you asked for, tell us first and we will look into it. Refund requests outside the 7-day window are decided case by case by Alex.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. We would rather fix it directly and quickly.
