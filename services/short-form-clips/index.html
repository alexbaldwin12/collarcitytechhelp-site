Coming soon: turn one video you own into 10 captioned vertical clips. · Planned price: $149 one-time

You filmed a video. Maybe a walk-through of your shop, a how-to, or you talking about your work. Most of it never gets seen because cutting it into short clips takes time.

What is included

  • 10 vertical clips, 30 to 45 seconds each
  • Cut from one video you own, up to 60 minutes
  • Captions burned into every clip
  • A hook title for each clip
  • Caption text and 3 to 5 hashtags for each clip, for TikTok, Reels and Shorts
  • A clip list by email so you can see what each clip covers
  • Delivery by download link within 5 business days
  • One round of small fixes, such as a caption typo, within 7 days

How it works

  1. Join the waitlist and tell us about your business. We email you when the service opens.
  2. Once it is open, you pay and fill out the intake form with a shared link to your video. Never send a password.
  3. We check the form and confirm you own the video and the music in it.
  4. We transcribe the video and pick the 10 strongest moments that make sense on their own.
  5. We cut them to vertical, add captions and hook titles, and write the caption and hashtag text in your tone.
  6. Alex reviews the batch. Within 5 business days you get a download link and the clip list by email.
  7. You post the clips on your own accounts, when you like.

What is not included

  • Posting or scheduling for you. We never log in to your accounts.
  • Other people's footage, stock video, or copyrighted music
  • Added music, sound effects, or voiceover
  • A second source video or more than 10 clips
  • Full video editing or motion graphics
  • Any promise of views, followers, or sales

Common questions

Can I use a video with music in it?

Only if you own the music or have the right to use it. We cannot use copyrighted songs or other people's content. The intake form asks you to confirm you own the video and the music in it.

Do you post the clips for me?

No. We never log in to your accounts. You get a download link and the caption text, and you post on your own.

Will the clips get me views or customers?

We cannot promise that, and nobody honestly can. We give you clips that are cut cleanly, captioned, and ready to post.
