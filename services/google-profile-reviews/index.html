Google Business Profile + Review Management

Coming soon: a tidy Google listing and calm, thoughtful review replies, written for you each month. · Planned price: $129/month

Your Google listing is often the first thing a new customer sees. In the first month we write a profile checklist covering categories, services, description, hours and a photo plan. After that, each month you get review-request wording, a reply draft for each review you paste in, a short review summary and 4 Google posts.

You copy and paste everything yourself. We never log in to your Google account or any other account.

How it differs from the $99/month Online Presence plan: that plan gives you 4 Google posts, 8 social posts and reply drafts. This plan goes deeper on your Google profile and reviews and does not include social posts. You may prefer one or the other, and we are happy to tell you which fits your business.

What is included

  • First month: profile checklist covering categories, services, description, hours and a photo plan
  • A note on Google Q&A: Google has been changing how Q&A works on profiles, so we put common answers in your description and services instead
  • Monthly review-request scripts: a text, an email and QR card wording, written to follow Google's rules with no incentives
  • A reply draft for every review you paste in
  • A monthly review summary with counts, themes and what to fix, based only on the reviews you paste
  • 4 Google Business Profile posts each month
  • Delivered by email within 3 business days of your monthly form

How it works

  1. Join the waitlist. We will email you when the service opens.
  2. Fill out the intake form with your business details and the reviews you want answered.
  3. We draft your pack and check it before sending. Anything we are unsure about is marked as a note for you to fill in.
  4. Within 3 business days of your form, you get the pack by email.
  5. You copy, paste and publish on your own Google profile. Cancel anytime by replying cancel.

What is not included

  • No logging in to your Google profile or any account. You publish everything yourself.
  • No scanning or checking of your website
  • No buying, trading or removing reviews
  • No guarantee of rankings, more reviews, more calls or more customers
  • No social media posts (the Online Presence plan covers those)
  • No legal, health or financial claims in your copy
  • No QR code image files or printing, only the wording

Common questions

Do you need my Google password?

No. We never ask for passwords or logins and never log in to your account. We write the text and you paste it in yourself.

Can you get me more five-star reviews?

We cannot promise any result. We write polite review requests that follow Google's rules, with no incentives and no asking only happy customers. What people write is up to them.

Should I pick this or the $99 Online Presence plan?

Online Presence includes 8 social posts along with Google posts and reply drafts. This plan adds a profile checklist, review-request wording and a review summary but no social posts. If reviews and your Google listing bring in most of your customers, this one may fit. If you also want social media content, Online Presence may fit better.

What if I do not get many reviews?

That is fine. The summary is based only on what you paste, so with few reviews it will be short and will say that the sample is small.
