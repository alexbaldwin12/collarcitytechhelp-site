Common questions

Do you need my Google password?

No. We never ask for passwords or logins and never log in to your account. We write the text and you paste it in yourself.

Can you get me more five-star reviews?

We cannot promise any result. We write polite review requests that follow Google's rules, with no incentives and no asking only happy customers. What people write is up to them.

Should I pick this or the $99 Online Presence plan?

Online Presence includes 8 social posts along with Google posts and reply drafts. This plan adds a profile checklist, review-request wording and a review summary but no social posts. If reviews and your Google listing bring in most of your customers, this one may fit. If you also want social media content, Online Presence may fit better.

What if I do not get many reviews?

That is fine. The summary is based only on what you paste, so with few reviews it will be short and will say that the sample is small.
