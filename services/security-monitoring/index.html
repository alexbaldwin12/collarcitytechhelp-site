Common questions

Does this scan my website?

No. It never touches your website. It only reads public email-domain records, the kind anyone can look up.

Will it tell me if I have been hacked or my data leaked?

No. It does not monitor for breaches or check for malware. If you think you are being hacked right now, contact your bank and IT provider and report it at ic3.gov.

Why would I want this?

SPF, DKIM and DMARC help stop other people sending fake email that looks like it came from your business. They can change when you switch email tools or someone edits your DNS. A monthly look helps you notice that.

What if you cannot find my DKIM record?

We check common selector names only. If we do not find one, it may still be set up. We will say that plainly and suggest asking your email provider to confirm.
