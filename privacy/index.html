Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-09. Collar City Tech Help, Mail: PO Box 182, Waterford, NY 12188.

Plain-English summary, not legal advice. Have a qualified professional review before relying on it.

Who we are

Collar City Tech Help is a small online business in Troy, New York, owned and run by Alex Baldwin. We help small businesses with plain-English security guidance and online presence content. Our work is designed and built by Alex using modern tools, including AI. See the site footer for our full disclosure.

This page explains what information we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect and why

We only collect what we need to answer you and deliver what you bought.

  • Security quiz: the quiz runs in your web browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you choose to type in your email and ask for your results. If you do, we receive your email address, your quiz score, and which tips were shown.
  • Email opt-in: if you give us your email for the checklist or quiz results, we use it to send what you asked for and a small number of follow-up emails about our services. Every email has an unsubscribe or opt-out line.
  • Order forms: the Security Scorecard questionnaire and the monthly content form collect your business name, email, website or email domain, and your answers about your business. We use them only to prepare your report or content.
  • Payments: you pay through Stripe. We see your name, email, the amount, and the order status. We never see or store your card number.
  • Emails you send us: we keep the messages and any files you attach so we can support you.

Services that handle data for us

We use a few outside services. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) carries our website form entries to our Gmail inbox.
  • Stripe processes payments. Card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay through our secure Stripe checkout.
  • Gmail (Google) is where we send and receive email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most hosts, it may keep ordinary server logs such as IP addresses.
  • AI tools help me work faster and keep prices low. We do not paste passwords or account numbers into them, and we ask you never to send any.

Cookies and analytics

As of the date above, our own site pages do not load analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies, and we do not use third-party scripts on them. The only outside connections the pages make are your clicks to Stripe checkout and form submissions to FormSubmit. If we ever add analytics, we will update this page first.

Links on our pages to other sites, such as Stripe, CISA, or Google, are governed by those sites' own policies.

What we never ask for

We never ask for your passwords, bank or card numbers, or ID numbers. We never log in to your accounts or scan your website. The only technical check we run is a public DNS lookup of your own business email domain. If you send a password or account number by mistake, tell us, change it right away, and we will not copy it anywhere.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising by other companies. We share it only with the services listed above as needed to run the business, or if the law requires it.

How long we keep things

We keep order records and related emails for as long as needed for tax and record-keeping for tax and bookkeeping reasons. Form answers and report files are kept for only as long as needed to do the work and answer questions after your last order, then deleted. Email list contacts stay until you opt out, and we keep your address on a do-not-contact list afterward so we do not email you again.

Your choices: access, correction, deletion

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete it. Email collarcitytechhelp@gmail.com or write to Collar City Tech Help, PO Box 182, Waterford, NY 12188. Please write from the email address you used, so we can confirm it is you. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can. We may need to keep some records, such as payment records, where the law requires. To stop marketing email, reply 'stop' or use the opt-out line in any message.

Children

Our services are for business owners and staff. They are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child sent us something, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will say so on this page.
