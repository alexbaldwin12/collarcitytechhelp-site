    What is in the house

    Water
    Stone jetty for swimming and tying up a boat. A canoe and two paddles on the rack. Life jackets by the door in every size.
    Warmth
    A woodstove with split wood in the shed. Baseboard heat in every room. Extra blankets in the loft.
    Kitchen
    Gas stove, full fridge, kettle and drip coffee. Pots, pans and plates for five.
    Sleep
    One bed, two daybeds and two loft mattresses. Linens and towels are here when you arrive.
    Outside
    A balcony off the bedroom, a fire ring, and parking for two cars.
    Connected
    Wifi at the table and in the loft. Cell signal is patchy on the point. One TV, in the bedroom.

    Where it is

    On the north-east shore, at the end of a gravel drive off County Road 12. The village is a short drive and the trail starts down the road.

    Questions people ask

    Can we bring the dog?

    Yes, up to two dogs. There is a $40 fee per stay (sample). Keep them off the beds and the daybeds. For any other animal, call first.

    Where do we park?

    Two cars fit on the gravel by the steps. A third can pull onto the shoulder of the drive. Please leave the turnaround clear so the plow and the propane truck can get in.

    How does check-in work?

    Check-in is from 4:00 pm. We text you a lockbox code the day before. Check-out is 10:00 am. If nobody arrives after you, we can often give a later check-out. Ask when you book.

    Is there wifi?

    Yes, at the dining table and in the loft. It is fine for email and a video call. We do not promise speeds, and cell signal is patchy on the point.

    What is the cancellation policy?

    Sample policy: full refund up to 14 days before arrival, half up to 7 days before, nothing after that. A real listing states the owner's own rules here.

    Is it safe to swim?

    The jetty is stone and the water gets deep fast past the end. Children need an adult at the water. Life jackets hang by the front door, and the lake emergency number is on the fridge.

    Can we have a fire?

    In the fire ring only, with the wood from the shed. When the county posts a burn ban we put a note on the door and the ring is off limits.

    Do you allow parties or events?

    No. Five guests overnight at most, and quiet hours are 10 pm to 7 am.
