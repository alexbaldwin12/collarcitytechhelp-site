A lake house near Birch Landing

Twelve minutes from the market and thirty steps from the water. Sleeps five, dogs welcome.

The short version

Kettle Point is a wood-and-glass house on the north-east shore of Little Kettle Lake, a twelve minute drive from Birch Landing.

Groceries, gas and a good coffee counter are in Birch Landing. The trailhead is four minutes from the drive and the public boat launch is seven. Everything else is the lake.

Questions

Can we bring the dog?

Yes, up to two dogs. There is a $40 fee per stay (sample). Keep them off the beds. For any other animal, call first.

Where do we park?

Two cars fit on the gravel by the steps. A third can pull onto the shoulder of the drive. Leave the turnaround clear so the plow and the propane truck can get in.

How does check-in work?

Check-in is from 4 pm. We text you a lockbox code the day before. Check-out is 10 am. If nobody arrives after you, a later check-out is often possible. Ask when you book.

Is there wifi?

Yes, at the long table and in the loft. It handles email and a video call. We do not promise speeds, and cell signal is patchy on the point.
