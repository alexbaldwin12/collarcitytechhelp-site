What to do, by season

Spring
Walk the trail to the falls while the ice goes out. Quiet roads, empty launch.
Summer
Swim off the dock, paddle the shoreline at dawn, eat outside until the mosquitoes win.
Fall
Maples turn the far shore red. Drive the ridge road and be back before the stove needs wood.
Winter
Ice fishing off the point once it is thick enough, and a long afternoon with a book by the stove.
Groceries
The Birch Landing market has what you forgot, and a counter that sells coffee early.
Rainy day
Puzzles and board games in the loft, and a map of the trails on the fridge.
