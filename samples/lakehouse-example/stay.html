What is in the house

Water
A wooden dock for swimming and tying up a boat. A canoe and two paddles on the rack. Life jackets by the door in every size.
Warmth
A woodstove with split wood in the shed. Baseboard heat in every room. Extra blankets in the loft.
Kitchen
Gas range, full fridge, kettle and drip coffee. Pots, pans and plates for five, and a long table to eat at.
Sleep
One bed, one daybed and two loft mattresses. Linens and towels when you arrive.
Outside
A covered deck, a fire ring and parking for two cars. An outdoor shower on the north wall.
Connected
Wifi at the table and in the loft. Cell signal is patchy on the point. One TV, in the living room.
