# Credits for lakehouse-example (Kettle Point, a fictional lake house)

Kettle Point, Little Kettle Lake, Birch Landing and every price, number, address and policy are made up. Example design for Collar City Tech Help. Not a real client.

## Photos and walk-in frames
Everything is cut from Poly Haven HDRI panoramas (tonemapped JPG), license CC0 (public domain), https://polyhaven.com/license. Credit is not required but given. Views were rendered by us (rectilinear crops from the panoramas), color-graded, and resized. The 72 walk-in frames are renders of a virtual camera moving through these panoramas, with dissolves between places. They are different real places, not one house.
