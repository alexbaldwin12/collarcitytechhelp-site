How a repair works

From keys in to keys back.

  1. 01

    Check in

    Drop the keys or wait in the lounge. We write down what the car is doing, since when and when it does it, in your words.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    A technician puts it on the lift and looks. Photos of what we find, good and bad, land on your phone while you are at work.

  3. 03

    Approve

    A written estimate with parts, labor and the total. Nothing starts until you say yes, and we call before anything is added.

  4. 04

    Fix

    The work is done to the maker’s specification by the technician who inspected it, then the car is road-tested before you hear from us.

  5. 05

    Pick up

    A text when it is ready. We walk you through what changed, hand over the old parts if you want them, and the repair carries a written warranty.
