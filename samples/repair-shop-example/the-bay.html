What each one does

Five stops on the tour.

Two-post lift

Raises the car about six feet so the tech can reach brakes, suspension and exhaust standing up. Every brake and suspension job starts here.

Book brakes

Tire and wheel station

Where tires are mounted and balanced. A wheel that shakes at 60 mph usually gets fixed here, in about an hour.

Book tires

Front desk and estimate counter

Where the keys go and where your written estimate gets explained, with the old parts on the counter if you want to see them.

Book a repair

Diagnostic bay

Scan tool, live data and a road-test checklist. A check-engine light is read here before anyone guesses at a part.

Book a diagnosis

Alignment rack

A drive-on rack that measures all four wheels. Run after suspension work or when the car pulls to one side.
