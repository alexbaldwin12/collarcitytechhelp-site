Questions about brakes
How do I know if my brakes are worn?

Squealing, grinding, a pedal that feels soft or a car that pulls to one side are the usual signs. We measure the pads and rotors and show you the numbers.

How long does a brake job take?

2 to 3 hours for most cars. Drop it off in the morning and it is usually ready by the afternoon.

Do I get a written estimate?

Yes, always, before any work starts.
