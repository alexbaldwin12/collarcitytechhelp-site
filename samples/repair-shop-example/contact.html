Questions

Before you come in.

Do you warranty the work?

Yes. Sample terms: 12 months or 12,000 miles on parts and labor, whichever comes first. Your real terms go here. The warranty is written on every invoice.

Do you have loaner cars?

Sample answer: we do not keep loaners. We can drive you home or to work within a few miles, or you can wait in the lounge. Your policy goes here.

How does an estimate work?

We look at the car first, then send a written estimate with parts, labor and the total. Nothing gets started until you say yes. If we find more, we call before we add it.

What is the diagnostic fee?

Sample: $129 for the first hour of testing on a check-engine light or a hard-to-find problem. If you go ahead with the repair here, the fee comes off the bill.

How do I pay?

Cards, cash and check at pickup. Your real payment options go here. We do not take card numbers over email or text.

Can I drop the car off early or late?

Yes. There is a key drop box by the door. Fill in a slip with your name, phone and what the car is doing, and we call you when the tech has had a look.

Do you work on every make?

Most cars, SUVs and light trucks. If we cannot do something well, we say so on the phone and point you to someone who can.
