Built to scale

Two bays or twelve, same page.

This section is a module. Bays and technicians are lists in one configuration file, so a two-bay independent shows two honest cards and a multi-location operation shows every bay under its location. No layout rework, no empty boxes.

Fewer bays means bigger cards and a tighter page. More bays means the board groups by location. The same lists drive the service tiles, the booking form and the fleet page.
