How an estimate works

Four steps. You decide at step three.

10 minutes

Drop-off or wait

Leave the keys, wait in the lounge or book a pickup. We record what the vehicle is doing, since when and under what conditions.

30 to 60 minutes

Inspection

A technician puts it on the lift, tests it and drives it. You receive photos of what we find, including what does not need work.

Same day

Written estimate

Parts, labor and the total in writing, split into what is urgent and what can wait. Nothing starts until you approve.

Most jobs the same day

Repair and verify

We do what you approved, road-test it, and return the old parts on request. If the scope changes, we call first.

Before you ask

Quick answers.

How does an estimate work?

A technician inspects the vehicle first. You then receive a written estimate with parts, labor and the total, by text or email. Nothing starts until you approve it, and if the work uncovers more, we call before anything is added.

What does the warranty cover?

Sample terms: 24 months or 24,000 miles on parts and labor for repairs, whichever comes first. The terms are printed on every invoice. Your real terms replace these.

Do you work on every make?

Most domestic, Asian and European cars, SUVs, light trucks and vans, hybrids included. For anything we cannot do well, we say so on the phone and point you to someone who can.

Can I wait, or should I drop it off?

Either. Oil, tires, batteries and inspections are scheduled as wait-while-you-service appointments. Brakes, suspension and diagnostics are usually drop-off, with a text when the technician has looked.

Do you offer a loaner or a ride?

Sample answer: we run a shuttle within a short radius and arrange rental pickup for longer repairs. Your real policy goes here.

What is the diagnostic fee?

Sample range: $125 to $165 for the first hour of testing on a warning light or an intermittent fault, more for luxury and European vehicles. When you proceed with the repair, the fee is credited to the invoice.

Not sure what it needs? Describe it.

Tell us what the vehicle is doing and we will tell you what it needs, and the range it falls in.
