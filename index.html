What we do

Basic security checkup

One phishing email can take down a small business. We make your business much safer than before by checking:

  • Passwords and a password manager you own
  • Two-factor login on email, banking, and social media
  • Automatic backups of your important files
  • Computer and phone updates
  • Your business Wi-Fi
  • A short talk for staff on spotting phishing emails

AI time-savers

We set up simple AI tools that save you hours, then teach you to use them yourself:

  • Email: draft replies to common questions for you to review and send
  • Scheduling: an online booking link for customers
  • Social media: a month of posts from your photos and ideas
  • Google reviews: polite replies, written fast
  • Menus, flyers, and FAQs

Who it's for

Owners of small businesses in Troy and nearby towns (Lansingburgh, Waterford, Cohoes, Watervliet, Brunswick, East Greenbush, and Albany) who have 1 to 20 employees and no IT person. Big IT companies are built for bigger businesses, and we are a local neighbor who is good with tech. We explain every change before we make it.
