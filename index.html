What we do

We do two things for small businesses. Both come with a clear price and a plain explanation of every change.

A basic security checkup

One bad email can cause a lot of trouble for a small business. We check:

  • Passwords, and a password manager that you own
  • Two-factor login on email, banking, and social media
  • Automatic backups of your important files
  • Computer and phone updates
  • Your business Wi-Fi
  • How to spot phishing emails (we can talk with your staff, too)

Simple AI tools that save time

We set up easy tools, then teach you to use them yourself:

  • Email: draft replies to common questions, for you to review and send
  • Scheduling: an online booking link for customers
  • Social media: a month of posts from your photos and ideas
  • Google reviews: polite replies, written fast
  • Menus, flyers, and FAQs

Who it's for

Owners of small businesses with 1 to 20 employees and no IT person. That includes dental, insurance, accounting, and real estate offices, plus downtown shops, cafes, salons, and contractors.

We work in Troy first, and also in Lansingburgh, Waterford, Cohoes, Watervliet, Brunswick, East Greenbush, and Albany.
